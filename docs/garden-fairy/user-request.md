# PROJECT0 — HIGH-QUALITY 3D GARDEN FAIRY AVATAR

I want you to create a beautiful, premium-quality 3D garden fairy avatar based on the reference image I am providing.

**IMPORTANT: The reference image is the visual target. Do not simplify, cartoonify, or reinterpret the character.**

The final character should look as close as possible to the reference, including her proportions, facial features, hairstyle, clothing, wings, colors, textures, and overall quality.

## 1. VISUAL QUALITY — TOP PRIORITY

I do NOT want a basic, low-poly, blocky, flat, or generic browser-game character.

The target is a premium, professionally sculpted, stylized 3D fantasy character with the quality of a high-end animated movie or collectible fantasy figurine.

Use:
- Smooth, detailed organic geometry.
- High-quality PBR materials.
- Realistic fabric, leaf, hair, and skin textures.
- Soft subsurface scattering for skin.
- Detailed normal and roughness maps.
- Proper ambient occlusion and soft lighting.
- Beautiful iridescent transparent wing materials.
- Smooth, rounded facial features.
- Fine details that hold up when the camera zooms in.

Do not create a character from spheres, cylinders, cones, flat planes, or simple primitive meshes and call it finished.

## 2. CHARACTER DESIGN

Create a small, adorable adult garden fairy with stylized chibi proportions.

She should have:
- A slightly oversized head with large expressive green eyes.
- Soft, round cheeks with pink blush and tiny freckles.
- A tiny nose and sweet, mischievous smile.
- Long, delicate pointed fairy ears.
- Dusty rose-pink hair in a messy, voluminous bun.
- Loose strands of hair framing her face.
- Tiny white flowers and green leaves woven into her hair.
- A petite body with soft, natural proportions.
- A playful, feminine, whimsical appearance.

Her expression should be charming and full of personality, not blank or doll-like.

## 3. CLOTHING AND TEXTURES

Her outfit must closely match the reference.

Create an intricate woodland fairy outfit made from overlapping leaves, vines, flowers, and delicate cream-colored fabric.

The clothing needs real dimensional detail:
- Individual overlapping leaves with visible veins.
- Slightly curled and irregular leaf edges.
- Fine fabric folds and translucent lace.
- Tiny white daisies attached to the outfit.
- Thin vines wrapping naturally around her arms and legs.
- Leaf-covered sandals with delicate straps.

Do not simply paint leaf patterns onto a green dress.

The leaves and fabric should have actual geometry, material depth, texture, and realistic lighting responses.

## 4. WINGS

Her wings are one of the most important features.

Create four large, delicate fairy wings with:
- Iridescent pastel pink, lavender, blue, mint, and gold coloring.
- Thin, intricate golden veins.
- Semi-transparent membranes.
- Subtle sparkling details.
- Beautiful light transmission.
- Slightly curled organic edges.

The wings must look magical and delicate, like stained glass mixed with butterfly wings.

They should not look like flat, solid-colored triangles or basic transparent planes.

## 5. POSE AND PRESENTATION

The fairy should be standing upright in a relaxed, playful pose.

- Full body visible from head to toes.
- Arms relaxed and slightly away from her body.
- Feet positioned naturally.
- Wings fully visible behind her.
- Facing mostly forward with a slight three-quarter turn.
- A cute, confident expression.

She should be a standalone character, without a garden, floor, scenery, props, or background.

First inspect the existing Project0 avatar system and determine what 3D formats, animation systems, rendering libraries, and asset-loading methods it supports.

Use a proper 3D character asset, preferably GLB/glTF if supported.

Prioritize an actual detailed 3D mesh with appropriate textures, rather than trying to procedurally recreate the character using simple Three.js primitives.

If suitable asset-generation tools or a modeling pipeline are available, use them.

The avatar must:
- Be viewable from all directions.
- Maintain its quality when zoomed in.
- Have properly attached wings and clothing.
- Use optimized textures without visibly sacrificing detail.
- Load efficiently in Project0.
- Be prepared for future idle, walking, and flying animations.

Before presenting the character as complete, compare the result directly against the reference image.
